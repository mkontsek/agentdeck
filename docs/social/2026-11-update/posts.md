## LinkedIn

The hardest part of working with several AI agents isn't opening more terminals. It's keeping track of their work without losing your own train of thought.

I've been improving AgentDeck, my open-source macOS workspace for AI coding agents.

The latest update gives subagents their own mini-windows, with visible connections to their parent and an owned browser alongside the terminal. You can move, resize and expand the workspace around the task.

The less visible changes matter just as much: worker updates go through a separate inbox, preserving unfinished prompts. Background panes don't take keyboard focus. Resizing respects your font setting, and terminal output causes less UI churn.

It runs the real CLIs—Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and others—with your existing accounts and configuration.

The animation shows the workflow with synthetic content. Source and setup instructions are in the first comment.
